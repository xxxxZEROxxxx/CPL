A fully custom-designed and independently built rechargeable portable light based on the **STM32F103C6T6** microcontroller. The entire product was developed from the ground up, including the circuit design, PCB design, firmware, component assembly, soldering, testing, and final hardware integration.



The firmware is developed in HAL, with the STM32 handling lighting control, user input, power management, and PWM-based brightness control.

### Key Features

* 5uA of energy drain in Standby mode
* **Adjustable brightness** using STM32 PWM control.
* **Adjustable color temperature**, allowing the light output to be tuned for different lighting conditions.
* **5-way control switch** for intuitive adjustment of brightness and color temperature.
* **Low-power sleep operation**, with measured sleep current of approximately **0.5 µA**, significantly reducing battery drain during standby.
* Designed for **long-term battery operation**, with very low standby consumption allowing the device to remain dormant for extended periods without frequent recharging.
* Custom **battery management and charging circuitry** integrated into the design.
* **AO3400 MOSFETs** used for power switching and PWM control.
* Custom PCB and electronic circuitry designed specifically for the device.
